Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to StrandLab ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the StrandLab mobile application.

By using StrandLab, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address when you register, sign in with Apple, or sign in with Google.
  • Hair Profile Data: Hair type, porosity, path selection, quiz responses, and hair concerns you provide.
  • Photos and Videos: Hair progress photos you upload to your Hair Journal.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with features such as routines, product labs, and trackers.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status managed through RevenueCat and the App Store. We do not store payment card details.
  • Crash Data: Technical diagnostics to improve app stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To personalize your hair routines and product recommendations
  • To provide and improve core app features
  • To verify founding member status and manage premium access
  • To process and manage subscriptions via RevenueCat
  • To diagnose crashes and fix technical issues

4. Third-Party Services

StrandLab uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — Authentication, database, and photo storage
  • RevenueCat — Subscription and purchase management
  • Google Sign-In — Optional authentication method
  • Sign in with Apple — Optional authentication method
  • Amazon Associates — Affiliate program for product links

5. Affiliate Links Disclosure

StrandLab participates in the Amazon Associates affiliate program. When you purchase products through links in the app, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. All product recommendations are editorially curated and are not influenced by affiliate relationships.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption. Photos are stored in Supabase Storage. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data via the Profile screen
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To delete your account, go to Profile → Delete Account within the app.

8. Children's Privacy

StrandLab is not directed to children under 9 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 9. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
